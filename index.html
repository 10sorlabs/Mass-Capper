✳ GOOD QUESTIONS

The important
stuff.

Got another question? We're still shaping the product.

Is this a real AI video generator yet?+

Not yet. This website is an interactive concept demo. You can explore the order flow, but it does not generate or deliver videos or take payments.

What kind of pet photo works best?+

A clear, front-facing picture with your pet's face visible is the ideal starting point. We currently focus on dogs and cats.

How long will the video be?+

We're exploring short shareable formats around 10–30 seconds. The final available lengths will depend on the generation model and launch offering.

Can I write what my pet says?+

Absolutely. The plan is to let you provide your own message or choose a funny prompt that we turn into a script.

What happens to my uploaded photo?+

In this prototype, the image stays in your browser and is only used for the local preview. There is no server upload. A production service would need a clear privacy policy and secure storage.
