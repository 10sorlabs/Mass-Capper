✳ QUESTIONS, QUESTIONS

Good to
know.

We're building Pawvo in public, so here's where things stand.

Can I order a real talking-pet video right now?+

Not yet. This site is a product and design preview. The video-generation and checkout systems have not been connected, so no orders are accepted.

What pet photos work best?+

Use a sharp photo with your pet's face clearly visible and minimal obstruction. Front-facing portraits are usually easiest to work with.

Can I use my own script?+

Yes in the concept configurator: you can write a message or select a sample line and see it in a mock preview. Actual speech generation will be part of the full product.

Is the preview price final?+

No. All prices currently shown are indicative and may change before launch.

Will you use my photo to train AI?+

This prototype does not upload your photo to a server. The preview is displayed locally in your browser. A full privacy policy will be published before processing customer images.
